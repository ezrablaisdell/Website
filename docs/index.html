✳OUT OF THE DEEP A PRAYER OF CREATION
++THE DEEP / תְּהוֹםFORM ARISING FROM THE FORMLESS

I CREATION

Out of
the deep.

Before the light, You knew the deep.
You spoke, and darkness learned the dawn.
You hung the stars upon Your breath,
yet bent to lift the dust.

FROM NOTHING, A WORLD.
FROM DUST, A SOUL.FOLLOW THE PRAYER↓FIG. 01 / THE FIRST LIGHT
II EXILEORDER / DISORDER

And we
wandered.

We are that dust, still bearing breath,
still hiding east of Eden.
The heavens keep the paths You gave;
we lose the road to You.

III MERCY

Remember
the dust.

O God who heard the slaves at night,
divide the sea within us.
Let not the work of Your hands be lost.

Remember us.
And bring us home.

Amen.
